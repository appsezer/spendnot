Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data Collection and Use

"Spend Not" (the app) is designed to operate 100% offline. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, financial information, email addresses, or usage habits to any external servers.

2. Local Data Storage

Any goals, financial inputs, and settings you create within the app are stored strictly and entirely locally on your device's internal memory. If you uninstall the app, all associated data is permanently deleted from your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising software (SDKs) to track users or display ads.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can reach out to the developer via the support links provided inside the app.
